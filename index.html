Group 3

Unreasonable Searches and Seizures

About The Project

Our group wanted to make sure our brochure was easy for anyone to understand. For our publicity materials, every team member found an image that directly connects to our topic of unreasonable searches and seizures. We also double-checked all of our facts so that everything in the brochure is accurate and trustworthy.

Core Objectives

Maximize Accessibility

Simplify language and visual design to ensure the brochure is clear and easy to understand for a broad audience.

Enhance Visual Relevance

Select and incorporate topic-aligned images contributed by each team member to reinforce key concepts.

Ensure Information Accuracy

Fact-check all historical, legal, and conceptual details to guarantee the content is accurate and reliable.
